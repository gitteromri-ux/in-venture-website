Roadmaps

Five roadmaps. One route to market.

A roadmap is a permanent page, not a blog post. Each one states what we believe, which Sumitomo business unit carries it, and which companies we have already backed against it.

Roadmap 01

Mobility

Vehicles are becoming software platforms with a legal driver problem. We back the layers that let a machine operate at distance and stay accountable: teleoperation, sensing, and the consumer-engagement data that moves with a person rather than a device.

Ottopia · Remote Operation of Vehicles Anagog · Acquired

Route to market

Sumitomo's Transportation & Construction Systems unit, plus the group's automotive distribution and leasing businesses across Asia.

Roadmap 02

Energy

Decarbonising heavy industry is a cost-per-kilogram problem, not a sentiment problem. Our position is that green hydrogen becomes an industrial input the moment its production cost crosses the line against grey, and that the crossing will be engineered in a lab before it is legislated.

H2pro · Hydrogen Fuel Production

Route to market

Sumitomo's Energy, Mineral Resources, Chemical & Electronics unit and its Infrastructure unit, both operating at utility scale.

Roadmap 03

Digital Health

Diagnosis is being pulled out of the central laboratory and pushed to the point of care. We back the instruments and the interpretation layer together: the machine that reads the sample, and the software that turns raw genetic and haematological data into a clinical answer a physician can act on.

LiteBC · Blood analysis for the 21st Century Genoox · Acquired

Route to market

Sumitomo's Living Related & Real Estate unit, which reaches hospital operators, retail pharmacy and consumer health distribution in Japan and Southeast Asia.

Roadmap 04

Food & Agtech

Food systems fail at the seams, between grower, processor, logistics and shelf. Israel exports the sensing and the agronomic modelling; Japan operates one of the most demanding food supply chains on earth. We look for technology that survives contact with that chain, and we are actively building this roadmap.

Open for founders in this domain

Route to market

Sumitomo's Living Related & Real Estate and Infrastructure units, spanning agricultural inputs, cold chain and food retail.

Roadmap 05

IT & Cyber

Our largest concentration. Two forces run through it: enterprises are handing revenue and security operations to software agents, and computing itself is changing substrate. BCG has identified over 100 potential areas for quantum applications and estimated the market opportunity at 400 to 750 billion dollars per year, while only about 400 to 450 quantum companies exist today.

Classiq · Quantum Algorithm Design Bria · Generative AI Alta Onfire Cypago Variant Confetti GK8 · Acquired CardinalOps · Acquired Port0 · Acquired

Route to market

Sumitomo's Media & Digital unit, and the group's own digital transformation programme across roughly 1,000 group companies.
