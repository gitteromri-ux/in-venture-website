At IN Venture our strategic value for startups is based on promoting value creation between our portfolio companies and Sumitomo Corporation's global businesses. IN Venture's unparalleled direct access to the global resources of Sumitomo allows us to serve as agile dealmakers for startups and Sumitomo Corporation.

By placing corporate impact at the heart of our investment process, and through directly facilitating business between our startups and Sumitomo companies, IN Venture delivers impact beyond the reach of most investors.
